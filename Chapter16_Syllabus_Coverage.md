# Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610 — Chapter 16 coverage crosswalk

This teacher-created practice bank is mapped to Section 16 of the Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610 syllabus (2026–2028). It contains 104 Core questions and 33 Supplement-only questions (137 unique question IDs across the bank). The Core track contains the 104 Core questions; the Extended track contains those same Core questions first, then the 33 Supplement questions. Shared content may appear in both tracks. No question or original slide image is repeated within a selected track.

## Assessment design and limits

The question stems are teacher-created, application-, analysis- or evaluation-led prompts in Cambridge-style Paper 1/2 multiple-choice, Paper 3/4 structured/data-analysis and Paper 6 alternative-to-practical formats. A six-section review checked question demand, prompt overlap, slide-figure relevance and coverage; revised prompts keep their teacher-authored accepted-response rules. Exact duplicate IDs and normalised prompts are also rejected by the server-side track assembler.

All figures are original visual assets extracted from the five supplied PowerPoint decks. A server-side source-image registry assigns each image one figure number and displays it at most once within Core or Extended; later question parts may refer to that figure number.

**Drawing-skill exception required by the teacher:** the syllabus includes a flower drawing/labeling skill in its plant-reproduction content. At the teacher's request, this practice pack does not ask students to draw or sketch. It assesses the associated structures, labels, functions and adaptations through identification and interpretation of original PowerPoint figures instead. Therefore all Chapter 16 content topics are represented, but student-produced drawing skill is intentionally not assessed.
